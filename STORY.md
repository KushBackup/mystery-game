## Prologue

Armaan Khanna built Velvet Ember Spirits in the image of himself: expensive, theatrical, and impossible to challenge without looking joyless. He invited people into his orbit by borrowing their skill, flattering their taste, or offering them proximity to power. He kept them there by withholding payment, credit, or safety at exactly the right moment.

By the time the buyout with Meridien Beverage Group is about to close, Armaan has made at least ten people in the room plausible prime suspects and dozens more willing to believe the worst of him.

He plans to survive the buyout by sacrificing other people first.

He never gets the chance.

---

## The Public Case

The party takes place on 8 August 2026 at For the Record, a late-night listening bar in Panjim, Goa. Armaan invites ten close friends and tells each of them to bring their own people. That creates ten visible circles in the room before the game even starts.

At 10:12 PM Armaan performs the Last Light, his signature single-glass birthday ritual. The room sees the cube, the orange mist, and the toast. At 10:22 PM he collapses. Police lock all 51 guests inside.

What the room believes early:

- one of the ten central invitees planned it,
- their own table probably helped,
- and the projector blackout around 10:08 PM is the obvious opportunity window.

That belief is useful, but wrong.

---

## The Real Murder Method

The poison is aconitine, hidden in a replacement orange-oil atomizer used only to finish Armaan's personal Last Light. The shared alcohol supply is clean. The batch mixers are clean. The birthday cake is clean.

The trick is not just the toxin. The trick is that the conspiracy splits the method into distinct jobs so no single group looks complete on its own:

- Roddy knows the botanical carrier.
- Kiyaah knows the drink ritual and controls the one-serve finish.
- Oindrilla creates the 94-second blind spot and cloned QR access.
- Victoria lays down paperwork that points suspicion sideways.
- Sneha keeps the room thinking in separate clusters rather than as one network.

## The Killer Team

### Sneha Ganesh
Mastermind. Armaan planned to throw the Monday audit onto her shoulders if the buyout risk surfaced. She understands the whole financial architecture and becomes the person who sees that Armaan has written a future in which several people in the room burn so he can stay charismatic.

### Kiyaah Rose Raghuwanshi
Owns the private-bar ritual Armaan stole from her. She is the only person in the room whose access to the Last Light finish feels natural instead of suspicious.

### Victoria Vance
Understands paper, signatures, shell-company exposure, and exactly how to make a false trail survive first scrutiny. Her role is not poison; it is blame design.

### Roddy Faustus
Knows how to turn bitter-orange aroma into a delivery vehicle. He is the most technical member of the team, but the game is written so he does not become obvious too early.

### Oindrilla Chatterjee
Owns the systems layer: projector reboot, offline admin path, retired staff QR reactivation, and the ninety-four seconds in which the room looks away.

## The Five Innocent Prime Suspects

These five are meant to remain genuinely dangerous-looking deep into the game:

- **Tara Singhania**: counterfeit provenance and customs exposure
- **Tanvi Vartak**: layout manipulation and pattern-tracking obsession
- **Rishi Raj Rahul**: public ego, private rage, buyer-call knowledge
- **Vinod Raghuwanshi**: political leverage and private meeting with Armaan
- **Anna Russo**: replica swaps, insurance fraud adjacency, archive access

They are not filler. The twist works because the room can make a coherent single-killer case against any of them before Round 5.

---

## The Hidden Twist

The room is supposed to spend Rounds 1 and 2 thinking, "Which suspect did it, and which of their people helped cover it up?"

Round 4 begins to break that model by revealing that Armaan had a Monday scapegoat plan targeting multiple people in different circles. Round 5 finishes the inversion with the admin trace and the recovered burner thread.

The solved answer is not:

- one killer,
- one dirty table,
- one emotional snap.

The solved answer is:

- five coordinated killers,
- spread across five different suspect circles,
- executing a plan designed to look like table loyalty rather than cross-room alignment.

That is the emotional payoff of the case.

## Round Payoffs

### Round 0
The room learns only the public shape: venue, victim, ritual drink, collapse, sealed room.

### Round 1
Accusations reinforce the false assumption that each suspect's own group may be protecting them.

### Round 2
Motive files make all ten prime suspects individually dangerous.

### Round 3
Evidence proves the poisoning lived in one finished drink, not the bottle or food supply.

### Round 4
Revelations expose the Monday scapegoat plan and show that Armaan was targeting multiple people at once.

### Round 5
The admin override trace and burner thread prove the killers coordinated across different groups.

### Round 6
The reveal names the killer team publicly. The killers themselves get the confession instead of the public overlay.

## Canon Truth

The satisfying answer the room should be able to reach by the end is:

**Sneha Ganesh led Kiyaah Rose Raghuwanshi, Victoria Vance, Roddy Faustus, and Oindrilla Chatterjee in poisoning Armaan Khanna through the finishing spray of his private birthday drink, while using a blackout, forged paperwork, and group-based social misdirection to keep suspicion trapped inside the wrong circles.**
