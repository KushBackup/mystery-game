## Paragraph to paste into the voice generator

Hey! This is Three Knives Out — the game we play at bars, with a whole room of friends and strangers. When you walk in, you answer six quick questions. What you're wearing, your first drink, that kind of thing. Hold on to that. Then your phone gives you a secret role. Most of you are Faithful. Three of you are Killers. And nobody knows who's who. At night, every phone goes dark at once. The Killers quietly pick someone to take. Everyone else hunts. The Doctor can save one person. The Detective can check two. In the morning, the whole room plays a quick game together. A word game, a drawing game, or tap to swim. The lowest score gets taken… and the Killers secretly rig who ends up last. Taken? You're not done. You come back as a ghost — and ghosts pass true clues to the living. Now the good part: the clues. Every clue describes a Killer. A dark top. Glasses. A gin. Check who matches. Ask around. Catch the lie. Every evening, the room votes someone out. And then the night comes back. Catch all three Killers, and the Faithful win. Let one slip through… and the Killers do. It's a bit of Mafia, Among Us, Pictionary and Cluedo — all on your phone, in one room. Come play with us sometime.

## Recording notes

- One file, `.mp3` or `.wav`. No music on it; the film adds its own sound effects.
- Natural pauses between lines are good. The animation is timed to your recording word by word, so any pace works.
- Aim for 75–90 seconds. Over 95 seconds starts to feel long on Instagram.
