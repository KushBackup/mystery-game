# Host Live Facilitation
## Velvet Ember: Birthday in Red

Use this at the event, not during writing. This is the fast operational layer over [HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md](HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md).

---

## Event Shape

- Venue: For the Record, Panjim, Goa
- Date in fiction: 8 August 2026
- Cast: 51 guests
- Prime suspects: 10
- Killers: 5
- Flow: 7 rounds, 1 host, printed clue cards, live discussion, repeated voting

Your job is not to perform the story. Your job is to keep the room pointed at the right problem at the right time.

---

## Pre-Game

### Material check
- 51 login cards
- 10 motive cards
- 7 evidence cards
- 6 revelation cards
- Host phone logged in and synced
- Round 3 and Round 4 case-file unlock buttons tested
- Emergency reference: [HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md](HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md)

### Room instruction
Say this plainly before you start:

"Your phone is your case file. The printed cards are not props. Read them, trade them, lie about them if you want, but do not lose them."

### Host watch-for
- Anyone stuck on login
- Anyone who has not opened Story / Incident Report before the first vote
- Any cluster that becomes isolated too early

## Round 0 · The Incident

### Objective
Get everyone oriented, then force a bad first vote before the room has enough information.

### Host actions
- Let the Round 0 briefing play
- Point players to Story, then Evidence → Case files
- Open a quick blind vote once people know the public facts
- Close the blind vote before the room can over-discuss it

### What to emphasize
- The venue is sealed
- Armaan's ritual drink matters
- Ten central invitees anchor the room
- Everyone has a reason to hate him

### If the room stalls
Ask:
- Who here saw the drink happen?
- Who thinks this is about one person and their own group?

### Advance when
People know the victim, venue, collapse time, and have cast a first bad vote.

---

## Round 1 · Accusations

### Objective
Make each suspect's own circle look dirty.

### Host actions
- Advance to Round 1
- Tell everyone to open their accusation card on Evidence
- Push verbal sharing, not silent reading

### What to emphasize
- These are witness claims, not verdicts
- Players can lie about what their accusation says
- The room should feel like each suspect may be protected by their own people

### Good prompts
- "Who got something about the bar?"
- "Who got something about paperwork?"
- "Who got something about the blackout?"

### Watch-for
If one suspect starts dominating too early, redirect toward a different accusation lane.

---

## Round 2 · Motives

### Objective
Make all 10 prime suspects feel individually dangerous.

### Host actions
- Hand out the 10 motive cards
- Mix distribution across groups rather than giving every suspect their own card
- Remind players the decoder is on Evidence

### What to emphasize
- This round is about why someone would want Armaan dead
- Motive alone is not enough
- The room should now be split between several plausible suspects

### If the room stalls
Ask:
- Who had the most to lose on Monday?
- Who had something to hide before tonight even started?

### Advance when
Players have stopped asking "who hated him?" and started asking "who could actually do it?"

## Round 5 · Finale

### Objective
Make the room name a team, not just a mastermind.

### Host actions
- Hand out the last 2 revelation cards
- Keep voting open or reopen it for final locking
- Use [HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md](HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md) if the room pokes at physical plausibility

### What to emphasize
- The burner thread proves coordination, not just suspicion
- Initials are functions before they are names

### Advance when
The room can articulate a five-part mechanism, even if they are still arguing over one or two names.

---

## Round 6 · The Reveal

### Objective
End cleanly, give the room the names, then give them the reconstruction.

### Host actions
- Close voting
- Optionally show the tally
- Trigger the reveal
- Let the room react
- Then point them into How it happened

### What to say after the screen lands
Use one sentence only:

"Read the reconstruction before you ask me questions. The answer is in there beat by beat."

### After reaction settles
If needed, open [HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md](HOST_QA_BRIEFING.md) for any objection handling.

Do not rescue the room from uncertainty too early. Your real job is to keep them solving the right shape of problem.
