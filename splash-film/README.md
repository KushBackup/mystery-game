# splash-film: the opening video

The first time a guest opens the page, their phone plays one 21.17-second
video, once per device, made of two hand-drawn films:

1. **The title film** ([splash.html](splash.html), 9.17 s, on white). An ink
   drop pops into a ring tunnel and the Astral Project sticker ("presents"),
   the vortex collapses to a seed that grows the Greenr sticker ("in
   collaboration with"), and a drop falls into a black sea under a red moon.
   For "The Deep Blue Case", crime-scene tape snaps across, CASE is stamped on
   in red, the title drips, a shadow with a red eye passes under the water,
   and a magnifier finds a fingerprint. An iris closes on white.
2. **The intro** ([intro.html](intro.html), 12.0 s). The iris opens on a
   rainy street at night. A guest drawn in the contact-photo creator's style
   walks along, eyes on her phone. She stops, taps faster, sweats, and a
   scribble of dread hangs over her. Her screen shows the morning game, a
   crash, the board, YOU at the bottom, LOWEST, and a glitch. She freezes,
   clutches her chest, buckles and falls, and the phone skids away, still
   lit. The camera goes into the phone and its light fills the frame with
   white.

The phone then fades to the set-up screen. Each film's brief and beat sheet sit at
the top of its HTML.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `splash.html`, `intro.html` | The two films: brief, drawings, scenes, timeline. The only files to edit |
| `core.js`, `studio.js`, `cels.js`, `render.mjs`, `package.json` | Copied from the hand-drawn-canvas-animation skill. Don't edit them here |
| `prep-logos.py` | Recolours the user's logos (`video/public/adtn/logo-*.png`, white for dark backgrounds) for white paper |
| `logos.js` | Generated by `prep-logos.py`: the logos as data URLs (a file:// image would taint the canvas) |
| `out/` | Renders (git-ignored). The shipped copy is `public/splash/deal.mp4` |

## Re-render and ship

```bash
cd splash-film
npm install                                   # once: puppeteer-core
python prep-logos.py                          # only if a logo changed
export CHROME=".../chrome.exe"                # any Chrome or Chromium; Playwright's works
node render.mjs splash.html --grid 30 --out out       # quick look at a film
node render.mjs intro.html --strip 198,42 --out out   # consecutive frames around the fall
node render.mjs splash.html --out out                 # the masters: out/splash.mp4
node render.mjs intro.html --out out                  #              out/intro.mp4
ffmpeg -y -i out/splash.mp4 -i out/intro.mp4 \
  -filter_complex "[0:v][1:v]concat=n=2:v=1:a=0[v]" -map "[v]" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 28 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -movflags +faststart -an ../public/splash/deal.mp4
```

## Things that must stay true

- **Seen once.** A device that has watched it doesn't see it again
  (`localStorage['astral.intro']`). After changing the video, bump `VERSION`
  in `src/os/splashFilm.js` so every device sees the new one. Keep
  `LENGTH_S` in `src/os/Splash.jsx` equal to the video's length.
- **Both films end white**, and the intro opens with the iris the title closed
  on, so the join is seamless.
- **Format.** 9:19.5 (1080 × 2340 units), shipped 720 px wide and shown with
  `object-fit: cover`. A 9:16 phone loses about 210 units off the top and the
  bottom, so keep everything that matters in y 300 to 2040.
- **The words** of the title film must match `SPLASH` in
  `src/data/killersCopy.js` and the pack's `caseTitle`: those are its
  screen-reader label.
- **Reduced motion** shows six stills (`STILLS` in `src/os/Splash.jsx`, in
  seconds into the joined video; the intro starts at 9.17). If a scene moves,
  re-pick moments where each beat's words and action are complete.
- **The intro's character** is drawn in the creator's style
  (`src/os/art/portraitArt.js`: paper skin, pencil-grey hair, the same top,
  trouser and shoe colours) but is her own code, posed side-on. The preset is
  woman, bob, glasses, yellow top, denim, white sneakers.
- **Never graphic.** No method, no blood on her, nothing that reads as
  self-harm. She is taken because her score was the lowest, which is the
  game's premise. Her phone shows no closing message (a "Who did this?"
  screen was cut at the user's request).
- **Fonts.** The lettering uses Segoe Print, Impact and Arial Black (Windows).
  Render on a machine that has them.
- **No sound.** The video is muted, so it can start without a tap.

## word.html — the Word tutorial clips

Six short hand-drawn clips that teach WORD, the first morning game, shipped
as `public/tutorial/word-1.mp4` … `word-6.mp4` with a poster each
(`word-N.jpg`, the clip's last frame). Each clip acts for 3–4 s and then
holds a fully composed frame for at least 1.2 s, because the app shows that
frame as the still and while players read. 4:3 (1440 × 1080 units), 640 ×
480, 24 fps, no sound. Shown about 290–360 CSS px wide with
`object-fit: cover`, so everything that matters sits in the middle 92 %.

| # | Clip | Starts | Length | Frames | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | wake | 0.00 s | 5.50 s | 0–131 | Four guests asleep at a café table; phones buzz and hop; they jolt awake, grab the phones; WORD writes itself on |
| 2 | cards | 5.50 s | 5.00 s | 132–251 | Four tent cards spin over one by one: POPCORN × 3, then "A snack ?"; Dev, above card 4, sweats, eyes darting |
| 3 | write | 10.50 s | 4.75 s | 252–365 | A pencil writes "Butter", a green tick, a 100 tag; a "Popcorn" note slides in, is scribbled out in red, droops, 0 |
| 4 | wall | 15.25 s | 5.50 s | 366–497 | Notes 1 Cinema, 2 Butter, 3 Microwave, 4 Kernel slap onto the wall; Dev peeks round its edge, reading; 5 Crunchy drifts in late |
| 5 | pick | 20.75 s | 5.25 s | 498–623 | A fingertip taps Cinema, Butter, Kernel and a gold star stamps on each; a second hand taps Butter; its tag goes 100 → 150 → 200 |
| 6 | board | 26.00 s | 4.75 s | 624–737 | THE BOARD: 300, 250, 200, 150 and Zed asleep on 0, phone face down; LOWEST thumps onto that row |

Files: `word.html` (brief, beat sheet, the shared drawing kit, the cast and
clip 4), `word-clips.js` (clips 1, 2, 3, 5, 6), `word-font.js` (Patrick
Hand as a data URL, generated by `make-font.mjs` from
`public/fonts/patrick-hand.woff2`; the film waits for it before
`defineFilm`). The four guests are the same in every clip: Ada (bob,
glasses, yellow), Ben (tufts, denim), Cleo (curls, sea blue), Dev (bun,
pink; the faker, marked only by acting). Zed only appears on the board.

```bash
cd splash-film
node make-font.mjs                                  # only if the font file changes
export CHROME=".../chrome.exe"
node render.mjs word.html --grid 36 --out out       # quick look
node render.mjs word.html --out out                 # master: out/word.mp4, out/word-frames/, out/word-contact.jpg
# cut the six clips from the PNG masters (frame-exact) and take each last frame as its poster
F=out/word-frames; n=1
for seg in 0:132 132:120 252:114 366:132 498:126 624:114; do
  s=${seg%%:*}; c=${seg##*:}; last=$(printf "%04d" $((s+c-1)))
  ffmpeg -y -framerate 24 -start_number $s -i $F/%04d.png -frames:v $c \
    -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 28 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -an \
    ../public/tutorial/word-$n.mp4
  ffmpeg -y -i $F/$last.png -q:v 4 ../public/tutorial/word-$n.jpg   # word-2 used -q:v 5 to stay under 60 KB
  n=$((n+1))
done
```

If a clip's length changes, change the `timeline` in `word.html` and the
frame ranges in the loop together (each range is start:count at 24 fps).

- **The example is never a live pair.** POPCORN / "A snack" must never be a
  `wordPairs` entry in `src/data/packs/*.js`: a Killer who had watched the
  tutorial would be handed the word. Its hint must not be a live hint either
  (Cashew's was "A snack" and became "A nut"). The same example is used in
  `src/data/wordTutorial.js` and the Help app; change all of them together.
- **Colour.** Gold only for stars and points, red only for the X and
  LOWEST, green only for the tick. The faker is never colour-coded.
- **Never graphic.** Zed is asleep and stamped LOWEST; nothing more.
