Astral Project · The Murder Mystery Experience · Killers Night

A whole night, one phone at a time.

This guide follows one complete game, played on real phones against a test server, and shows what every player saw at every beat. Each column is one guest. Their role is shown on the header and the border colour: red for a Killer, teal for the Doctor, blue for the Detective, pale for the Faithful. A grey screen is a ghost.

The game in one minute

It is Mafia on phones. Everyone's phone becomes a retro iPhone with a cursed app called DEEP BLUE. Each morning it rings and the whole room plays a short game. The lowest score is taken by the deep.

Three Killers secretly rig a score so their chosen victim is last. The Faithful, a Doctor and a Detective work out who they are from clues that describe the Killer's real looks (top colour, glasses, shoes, drink, birthday, siblings), then vote one person out each day. Taken and voted-out guests become ghosts and keep playing.

  1. Night. The Killers vote on a victim; the others pick a move.
  2. Alarm and morning game. Word, Sketch or the Run.
  3. The board. A synced reveal: the lowest score is taken.
  4. Investigation. Clues, Contacts, and real conversation.
  5. The Round Table. A vote to log someone out.

After the last day: two final votes, then the finale tells the whole story back.

Arrival

The first time a phone opens the game it sets itself up. Nothing here is a role yet, which is the point: nobody knows whether they will want to lie, so they answer straight.

In the room. Guests drift in over ten minutes, scan a QR code and set their phone up at their own pace. Nobody has a role yet, so nobody has a reason to lie: they answer the six questions straight, and those answers become public.

The deal

The host presses Deal. Every phone gets a text from DEEP BLUE with a sealed role.

In the room. The host presses Deal and every phone buzzes within the same second. People shield their screens, tap their role open, read one line and close it. A sealed message looks identical on every phone, so a glance at a neighbour shows nothing. A few Killers notice a new group chat appear.
